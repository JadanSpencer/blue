How a project runs

The same five stages whether it is a single flyer or a full brand launch.

  1. Brief

    Tell us what you need through the project brief, WhatsApp or a call.

  2. Concept

    We come back with a direction and first concepts to react to.

  3. Refine

    You review, we revise, until it is exactly right.

  4. Produce

    Final artwork, print, build or broadcast, handled by our team.

  5. Support

    We stay on for reprints, new campaigns and whatever comes next.

Common questions

Answers before you call, in plain ink.

Q1What does a project cost?

It depends on what the job needs, and you will know before we start. Every project gets a written quote once the brief is clear, so nothing is billed by surprise. If the number does not work for you, we will tell you what it would take to bring the job inside your budget.

Q2How long does a project take?

A flyer can turn around in days; a full brand takes weeks. We agree the timeline in writing at the brief stage and keep you updated through each stage of the run.

Q3Do you only work in Mandeville?

Mandeville is home, but the work travels. We have delivered for clients across Jamaica, from resort brands to parish credit unions, and production is handled under one roof either way.

Q4What should I bring to start?

Nothing polished. Bring the problem you want solved, your deadline and a sense of budget. The project brief walks you through the rest, or simply call and tell us the story.

Q5Do you design only, or produce too?

Both, and that is the point. We design in four dimensions and then carry the work through print, build or launch ourselves, so what you approve is what actually goes out into the world.
