A blue flame is the hottest part of the fire. That is the standard we set in 2005, when Blue Ember Concepts broke away from the limits of the traditional design shop.

Today our team brings over twenty years of experience in identity, marketing, brand development, décor and creative building. We work best with companies that want something outside the box, and we stay with them as they grow.
